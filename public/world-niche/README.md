# World Niche portfolio preview

The portfolio features Discover from World Niche 1.1, captured November 24, 2026.
The portrait and unfolded layouts show the same globe centered on Morocco.

- `discover-cover.webp` and `discover-duo.webp` are resized copies of
  `01-discover.png` and `duo-01-discover.png` from the
  [1.1 source screenshots](https://github.com/RedaB2/WorldNiche/tree/d3a1d3a675aeb12313fd1957f8e790cc0ee30911/marketing/app-store/1.1/source).
- `duo-grip.webp` and `duo-leaf.webp` come from the
  [landing site's hand-held Duo frame](https://github.com/RedaB2/WorldNiche/blob/d3a1d3a675aeb12313fd1957f8e790cc0ee30911/apps/web/landing-site/assets/hand-duo.png).
  The split follows the hinge at x=510 on the original 1024×1536 canvas.
  The palm is tucked under the stationary half so the grip works at every fold angle.
- `logo.png` is the [original planet logo](https://github.com/RedaB2/WorldNiche/blob/374af6c5fc390d93fc4dd9b759df2242ac0db4dd/apps/web/landing-site/assets/logo.png),
  tinted green with CSS.
- `download-on-the-app-store.svg` is Apple's English download badge from the
  [World Niche landing site](https://worldniche.app/landing/assets/download-on-the-app-store.svg).
