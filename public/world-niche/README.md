# World Niche portfolio preview

The portfolio features Discover from World Niche 1.1, captured November 24, 2026.
The portrait and unfolded layouts show the same globe centered on Morocco.

- `duo-closed.webp` and `duo-open.webp` contain the portrait and landscape
  [Discover captures](https://github.com/RedaB2/WorldNiche/tree/d3a1d3a675aeb12313fd1957f8e790cc0ee30911/marketing/app-store/1.1/source).
  Both frames use the rounded metal rim from the original
  [Duo artwork](https://github.com/RedaB2/WorldNiche/blob/d3a1d3a675aeb12313fd1957f8e790cc0ee30911/apps/web/landing-site/assets/hand-duo.png).
  During the fold both inner panels sample the same texture. At full extension
  the browser paints that texture once across the entire device.
- `duo-hand.webp` preserves the fingers, palm and wrist from the
  [portrait hand artwork](https://github.com/RedaB2/WorldNiche/blob/d3a1d3a675aeb12313fd1957f8e790cc0ee30911/apps/web/landing-site/assets/hand-phone.png).
  The old phone was masked out. The thumb overlaps the fixed rim and the moving
  panel passes in front of the fingertips.
- `logo.png` is the [original planet logo](https://github.com/RedaB2/WorldNiche/blob/374af6c5fc390d93fc4dd9b759df2242ac0db4dd/apps/web/landing-site/assets/logo.png),
  tinted green with CSS.
- `download-on-the-app-store.svg` is Apple's English download badge from the
  [World Niche landing site](https://worldniche.app/landing/assets/download-on-the-app-store.svg).
